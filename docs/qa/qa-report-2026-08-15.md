### [P2] 20 migration duplicate prefix groups (carry-over from 2026-05-30)

**Where:** `supabase/migrations/*.sql`
**Symptom:** 20 groups of 2–3 files share the same `YYYYMMDD_NNN` prefix. Non-deterministic apply order on a fresh Supabase restore.
**Groups:** `20260504_{001×3, 002×2, 003×2}`, `20260505_001×3`, `20260506_{001×2, 013×2}`, `20260507_{009×2, 011×2, 012×2}`, `20260508_{001×2, 002×3, 003×2}`, `20260517_001×3`, `20260522_004×2`, `20260601_001`, `20260605_{003×3, 004×3}`, `20260606_003×2`, `20260608_003×3`, `20260609_001×2`
**Suggested fix:** Rename later files to next available sequence (`_002`, `_003`, etc.) and verify no FK/ordering dependencies before renaming.
**Why not auto-fixed:** Migration renames require re-applying statements on all Supabase environments; human must verify before rename.

### [P2] 480 `<button>` elements without explicit `type=` attribute

**Where:** `apps/restaurant-web/src` (primarily form components)
**Symptom:** Buttons inside or adjacent to `<form>` elements without `type="button"` or `type="submit"` default to `type="submit"`, which can trigger unintended form submissions. Most appear intentional (`phone-login-form`, checkout CTAs) but proximity to forms makes some risky.
**Most at risk:** `item-detail-actions.tsx` quantity buttons — confirmed they have explicit `type="button"` ✅. Risk is lower than the count suggests.
**Suggested fix:** Add `type="button"` to any button that should NOT submit a form. At scale (480 instances) this is a phased manual pass, not an auto-fix.
**Why not auto-fixed:** Exceeds 50-line cap; incorrect `type` on a submit CTA would silently break checkout.
